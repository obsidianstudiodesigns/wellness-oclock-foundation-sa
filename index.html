Meet the Founder

Nunu Motaung

B.SOC.SC (SW) UFS

Nunu Motaung is a passionate and dedicated mental health professional with a deep commitment to empowering individuals, families, and organisations to thrive holistically. As the founder of Wellness O'clock Foundation SA, she brings warmth, expertise, and evidence-based practice to every interaction.

Based in Emalahlenu/Witbank, Nunu serves clients across South Africa, offering a safe and confidential space for healing, growth, and transformation.
