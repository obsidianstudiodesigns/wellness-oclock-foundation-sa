Meet the Founder

Nunu Motaung

B.SOC.SC (BSW) UFS — Founder & CEO

Nunu Motaung is a Registered Social Worker in private practice and a licensed Mental Health & Wellness Therapist with 14 years of experience. She completed her Bachelor of Social Work at the University of the Free State in 2009, majoring in Social Work and Psychology.

Her career spans government (Social Development), NGOs (FAMSA & CMR), and the mining industry, where she served as Employee Wellness Advocate and EAP Coordinator at Glencore — work that earned her the Best Employee Wellness Advocate award at the Glencore Coal SA CEO Awards.

She founded Wellness O'clock Foundation SA in April 2022 with a simple mission: to help people find balance between life, family, and work. Based in Emalahleni/Witbank, Nunu serves clients across South Africa.

"Balance is not something you find, it's something you create."
Registered Social Worker Mental Health Therapist EAP Professional Keynote Speaker Wellness Advocate

Reschedule & Refund Policy

These terms apply to all therapy sessions and wellness events. Booking with us means you accept them.

🔁

Rescheduling

  • Reschedule requests must be made at least 24 hours before your session. Failure to do so results in forfeiting the fees paid.
  • Only one reschedule is allowed per booked session.
  • There are no refunds on rescheduled sessions.
  • Arriving late means you use the remainder of your timed session — sessions cannot run over.
  • Missing a session without notice (no-show) forfeits the full fee.
🏥

Medical Aid Bookings

  • Medical aid clients must book in advance of the appointment date — authorisation takes administrative time.
  • No cash refunds on medical aid bookings.
  • A booking can only be reversed back to your medical aid within one week of booking. After one week you may reschedule instead.
  • If your medical aid does not pay within the month that services were rendered, you are personally liable for the full amount.
💵

Cash Paying Clients

  • Refunds may be requested 5 days before your scheduled appointment.
  • Approved refunds are paid within 5 working days.
  • A 50% cancellation fee is subtracted from the amount paid.
  • Bank or transaction charges are not recoverable and are deducted from the refund.
🎪

Wellness Events & WellCations

  • All wellness event bookings are non-refundable.
  • You may transfer your booking to another upcoming event instead.
  • Event bookings close on the advertised closing date — no bookings are accepted after it.
  • If we postpone or cancel an event, your payment carries over to the new date or an event of your choice.
✍️

How to Request

  • All reschedule and refund requests must be made in writing — WhatsApp or email — so the request is dated and recorded.
  • The time we receive your written request determines whether it falls inside the 24-hour or 5-day window.
  • A slot is only held once payment is confirmed.
🤝

If We Cancel

  • Should we need to cancel or move your session due to illness or an emergency, you receive a full refund or a free reschedule — your choice.
  • No cancellation fee applies when the change comes from our side.
  • Corporate and group bookings may carry separate terms, set out in your written quotation.

Questions about this policy? WhatsApp us on 073 902 7279 or email cathynmotaung@gmail.com before you book.
